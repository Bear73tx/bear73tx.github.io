Privacy Policy

This privacy policy applies to the Barmaiden app for mobile devices, together with any related services operated by Cyber Bear Studio (collectively, the "Application"). Cyber Bear Studio is hereby referred to as the "Service Provider".

What information does the Application obtain and how is it used?

The Service Provider does not require user registration and does not directly collect, log, or store personal information such as your name or email address. However, the Application uses third-party services—specifically Google AdMob (to display advertisements), Google Play Billing / Apple App Store (to process optional in-app purchases such as removing ads), and Unity (game engine functionality and diagnostics).

When the Application is used with an active internet connection, these third-party services may automatically collect certain information, including:

  • Device identifiers (such as your device's Advertising ID)
  • IP address (which may be used to estimate approximate location)
  • Device information (such as device model, operating system version, and language)
  • App and ad interaction data (such as ad impressions, clicks, and in-app purchase status)
  • Crash logs and performance diagnostics

This data is used to serve and measure advertisements, process and restore in-app purchases, prevent fraud, and maintain app stability.

Does the Application collect precise real time location information of the device?

This Application does not collect precise GPS or real-time location information about your mobile device. However, third-party advertising services (such as Google AdMob) may infer your approximate location (such as city or country) from your IP address to serve relevant ads.

Do third parties see and/or have access to information obtained by the Application?

The Service Provider does not sell your personal data. However, the Application integrates third-party services that collect and process data as described above in accordance with their own privacy policies:

  • Google Play Services & Google AdMob: https://policies.google.com/privacy and https://policies.google.com/technologies/partner-sites
  • Apple App Store (iOS): https://www.apple.com/legal/privacy/
  • Unity: https://unity.com/legal/privacy-policy

What are my opt-out rights?

  • Uninstall: You can stop all collection of information by the Application easily by uninstalling it from your device.
  • Personalized Ads: You can opt out of personalized advertising or reset your Advertising ID at any time through your device settings (on Android: Settings > Google > Ads; on iOS: Settings > Privacy & Security > Tracking and Apple Advertising). You may also permanently disable ads within the Application by purchasing the one-time "Remove Ads" in-app purchase.
  • Direct Communications: If you contact the Service Provider directly or voluntarily provide information by other means, you may request deletion of that information by contacting CyberBearStudio@gmail.com.

Security

The Service Provider does not operate user-account servers storing personal player data, and data transmitted by third-party SDKs integrated into the Application is encrypted in transit. However, no security system is completely secure. The Service Provider implements reasonable safeguards to protect systems and any data it holds.

Because the Service Provider does not maintain user accounts or store personal player data on its own servers, the risk of a data breach affecting your personal data held by the Service Provider is minimal. If a breach occurs involving any data you have voluntarily provided to the Service Provider, the Service Provider will notify you as required by applicable law.
